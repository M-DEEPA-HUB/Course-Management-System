Intro to UX Research

Learn how to plan interviews, run usability tests, and turn raw findings into decisions your team can act on.

About this course

This course walks through the full research cycle: framing a research question, recruiting participants, running moderated and unmoderated sessions, and synthesizing results into insights stakeholders actually use. You’ll leave with templates you can reuse on your next project.
